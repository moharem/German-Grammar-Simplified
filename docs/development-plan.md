# GGS: foundation pilot and expansion

The proposed course uses Schubert A/B/C as its coverage backbone, Hammer as an English explanatory reference, and IDS grammis for difficult cases. See [the research and full topic inventory](research/german-grammar-reference-a1-c2.md). Individual level placement is a pedagogical proposal.

## Start with one small topic

Proposed pilot: **people + sein + simple statements**, beginning with “I am” and “you are.” Explain noun, pronoun, verb and subject in plain English when needed. Introduce a question only after its statement structure is understood. This topic is proposed, not implemented in the scaffold.

1. Explain one meaning and its purpose with familiar vocabulary.
2. Show a complete worked example with the parts identified.
3. Offer guided practice with a visible model and optional hints.
4. Offer independent practice after the learner is ready; explain mistakes.
5. Revisit with a fresh example on a later visit.

Aim for a short adjustable session, initially around 5–10 minutes; this duration is a hypothesis. Allow replay, pause and explanation. Avoid testing vocabulary or prerequisites that have not been introduced. Side-by-side comparisons show whole sentences, changed pieces, meaning, use and the reason for choosing a form.

## Refine, then expand

Observe whether a true beginner can explain the idea, follow the worked example, and attempt a fresh sentence without confusion. Ask where pace, vocabulary or terminology caused difficulty. Improve those parts and retry. Do not promise an objectively “best” method from one person's feedback.

Once the first topic is usable, test a second foundation topic to check that the pattern transfers. Then expand in small batches, revisiting prerequisites and retaining topic-specific adjustments. Delayed performance matters alongside immediate answers. The earlier werden/würden/worden prototype stays available for reference, with development paused.

## Platform foundation

- Responsive React + TypeScript + Vite app, used in desktop/mobile browsers or installed as GGS.
- GitHub Pages deployment through Actions after tests and a production build.
- Production manifest, local icons/fonts and service-worker precaching for offline use after a successful first download.
- New versions prompt the learner to update instead of forcing a reload during practice.
- Existing trial progress remains local to the browser. No backend or account is introduced.
- English first; Arabic localization and right-to-left review follow stable content.
