# Trial lesson decisions

Revised on 5 October 2026 following feedback that the first version required too much reading and assumed German knowledge.

## Audience and presentation

Support beginners and returning learners through general everyday situations. Choose **English or German** at the start; switch without resetting progress. English mode provides actual interface and feedback translation, plus sentence meanings. Arabic fields and direction metadata support later localization.

Teach through **three visual memory cards**, with optional details:

- **werden:** an arrow. Three small tabs distinguish future, change of state, and an action performed on something. The following word determines the construction.
- **würde / würden:** a thought bubble. Imagined actions and polite requests. Show `ich würde` alongside `wir / Sie würden`.
- **worden:** a delivered parcel. `ist / sind + past participle + worden`; contrast completed passive with a changed state using `geworden`.

Sentence blocks and meanings make the patterns concrete. A recall control hides the sentence and formula before revealing them again. Formal grammar names, full conjugations and additional exceptions sit in an optional details panel.

## Short flow

Three diagnostic questions, one card board, six varied practice questions, up to two targeted extras, optional speaking, and a review showing one missed or assisted question at a time. A learner may end review early. Estimated 4–6 minutes; local saving supports multiple sittings.

Correct and incorrect responses receive immediate, concise feedback. Additional explanation is available under **Why?**. Distinct feedback addresses `wurde / würde`, subject agreement, and `worden / geworden`. Spelling normalization accepts ASCII umlaut alternatives without merging grammatical distinctions.

Grammar hints count as assistance. Sentence meanings and vocabulary translations do not: understanding a sentence is a prerequisite for a beginner's grammar practice. First practice attempts determine the result; diagnostic answers, speaking and review retries do not inflate it.

Extra questions target concepts with errors or assisted answers. Selection remains bounded, unique within a round and deterministic. A five-question revisit prioritizes unseen examples and recorded weak distinctions while retaining mixed practice. Next-day recall is suggested within the app, without notifications.

## Scope and limits

One React + TypeScript lesson, responsive visual cards, touch and keyboard controls, local persistence and optional browser audio. General everyday examples work for a broad audience. No account, backend or paid service is required.

Pictures and repeated recall are memory aids. This trial does not claim that a rule can never be forgotten, establish a learner's CEFR level or measure conversational fluency. See [research notes](research/trial-lesson-research.md) for the prior primary-source research and grammar checks.
