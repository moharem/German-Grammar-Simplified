# German Learning

A German refresher course for learners rebuilding their ability through short, active study sessions. The initial scope is one trial lesson on _werden_, _würden_, and _worden_.

## Language

**Topic**:
A German language concept explored through explanations and practice, such as the uses of _werden_, _würden_, and _worden_.
_Avoid_: Lesson, session

**Lesson**:
A guided learning experience for a topic, from a diagnostic through explanations and practice to a review.
_Avoid_: Topic, session

**Study session**:
A period the learner spends studying; a lesson may be completed across several study sessions.
_Avoid_: Lesson

**Diagnostic**:
A short opening set of questions that reveals which distinctions the learner currently understands and which need practice.
_Avoid_: Placement exam, certification test

**Exercise**:
An active task in which the learner responds to a German sentence or conversation and receives feedback.
_Avoid_: Explanation

**Feedback**:
A response to an exercise attempt that explains the answer and, when needed, the learner's mistake.
_Avoid_: Score

**Missed-question review**:
Practice focused on questions the learner answered incorrectly earlier in the lesson.
_Avoid_: Diagnostic, topic revisit

**Topic revisit**:
A later return to practice a previously studied topic.
_Avoid_: Missed-question review

**Language help**:
An explanation or translation the learner can reveal in a support language while the lesson remains primarily in German.
_Avoid_: Interface language
